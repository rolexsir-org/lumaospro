## Overview

LumaOS Pro is an Android home-screen launcher. It does not require an account and
has no app-operated backend, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. The app has no
`INTERNET` permission in its source manifest and does not itself upload the
launcher data described below. Some user-invoked Android platform services,
including speech recognition and Android backup, may process or transfer data
under the platform/provider's own terms and privacy policy.

## Data the app accesses or stores

To provide launcher functions, the app accesses installed, launchable app names,
icons, and package/activity identifiers through Android's launcher APIs. It
stores the user's settings and launcher setup on the device, including home and
dock layouts, folders, hidden or pinned app identifiers, themes, gestures, and
context schedules.

If enabled, local launch history records apps opened through the launcher. The
app also stores local diagnostics and optimization history, such as launcher
startup and frame measurements, the app's process-memory readings, and actions
the user runs. These measurements are used in the on-device diagnostics screens;
they are not sent by the app to a server.

The app does not request location, calendar, or contacts access. Optional
context-change notifications are generated by the app itself. If the user grants
Android Notification Policy Access and enables a Do Not Disturb context, the app
reads and changes the system interruption filter as described in the app; it
restores the prior filter when leaving that context where Android still reports
that the app owns the filter.

The optional Luma Glass Music widget needs Android **notification access** to
find and control compatible media sessions. Android grants a notification
listener the capability to receive all notifications, not just music. Luma's
listener does not inspect, store, or upload notification contents; it queries
active media sessions and temporarily processes player names, track titles,
artists, playback state, and available artwork to display the widget. The Android
widget host may cache the displayed widget content. The user can decline or
revoke notification access in Android Settings; the widget then cannot access
other players. This is separate from notification posting and Do Not Disturb
access.

Wallpaper Studio can copy a photo selected through the Android document picker
into app-private storage. The original is not deleted. The copy can retain
embedded metadata, including location metadata if the original contains it.
The app does not read that location metadata; it reads orientation to display
the image correctly. Copies are excluded from Luma's Android cloud backup and
device transfer, and can be removed in Wallpaper Studio. The in-app manual
backup also excludes photo copies and wallpaper favorites. When a user sets a
wallpaper, Android stores and presents its own copy and the system or device
manufacturer controls its crop and retention. The app requests that custom
photo wallpapers not be backed up by the system; device behavior may vary.

## Optional voice commands

Voice commands are user-triggered. The app launches Android's installed speech-
recognition activity and passes the spoken audio to that service. Depending on
the device and provider, recognition may happen on-device or the provider may
send audio to its own service. The provider's privacy policy and settings govern
that processing. The app receives the recognized text to carry out the requested
launcher command, processes it locally, and does not retain the transcript. You
can decline to use voice commands.

## Sharing and third-party processing

The publisher does not sell launcher settings, app lists, launch history, or
diagnostics. The app does not send those data to the publisher or other parties.

Two Android-managed flows are exceptions to purely on-device storage:

- **Android backup:** Android cloud backup may transfer app preferences and home
  layouts, including wallpaper-favorite identifiers, when backup is enabled on
  the device; imported wallpaper photos are excluded. Device-to-device transfer
  may also include diagnostics history. The user controls Android backup and
  transfer through system settings.
- **Speech recognition:** as described above, the selected Android recognition
  provider receives voice input when the user invokes a voice command. The
  provider's processing is governed by its own terms and policy.

The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Android system services
may process information under Google's or the relevant device/provider's own
terms.

## Retention and deletion

Launcher settings, imported photo copies, and diagnostics remain in the app's
private device storage until the user changes or clears them, clears the app's
storage in Android Settings, or uninstalls the app. The app provides an in-app control to clear
launch history. Android-managed backups may remain subject to the user's backup
settings and Google's/device maker's retention rules; manage or delete those
through the relevant system account or device settings.

## Security

The app stores its local data in Android app-private storage and relies on the
Android platform's application sandbox and backup controls. The publisher does
not operate an app server or a separate cloud database for this app. No claim of
additional end-to-end encryption is made.
